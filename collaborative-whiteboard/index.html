How Collaboration Works with Screen Sharing

Visual collaboration via screen sharing — the simplest and most universal method.

1

Open the Whiteboard

One person opens onlinewhiteboard.org — no login, loads instantly. This person becomes the facilitator of the session.

2

Start a Video Call & Share Your Screen

Open Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, or any video platform. Share your browser tab showing the whiteboard. All participants now see the canvas in real-time.

3

Draw, Discuss, and Build Together

The facilitator draws as the group discusses. Participants guide verbally or via chat. Anyone can take control and draw by requesting screen control (in Zoom) or switching roles.

4

Export the Shared Board

After the session, export the board as a PDF — a complete visual record. Share with all participants as collaborative meeting notes or lesson summary.

Collaboration Tip: Use multiple boards (one per agenda topic) and sticky notes for each participant's ideas. At the end, take a PDF export of all boards as your session documentation.
