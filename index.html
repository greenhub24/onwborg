Free Online Whiteboard — No Download, No Login Required
OnlineWhiteboard.org is a free, browser-based online whiteboard that runs directly in your web browser. No software to install, no account to create — open it and start drawing in seconds from any device, anywhere in the world. This interactive online whiteboard is built for teachers, students, remote teams, and anyone who thinks visually.
What Is an Online Whiteboard?
An online whiteboard is a digital canvas that lives entirely in your browser. Draw diagrams, sketch ideas, write notes, add shapes, upload images, and explain concepts on a clean interactive canvas — without installing anything or signing up. OnlineWhiteboard.org includes a full drawing toolkit: pen, calligraphy, highlighter, shapes, text, sticky notes, arrows, connectors, and image annotation. Once your session is done, export your work as a PDF, PNG, or SVG for sharing in presentations or virtual classrooms. Everything loads instantly, and your work stays private in your browser.
What Is a Virtual Whiteboard?
A virtual whiteboard replicates a physical whiteboard entirely in your browser. Instead of standing at a board with markers, you open a URL and draw with your mouse, touchpad, or touchscreen. Everything a real whiteboard does — write, erase, add images, zoom in — you can do on a virtual whiteboard, plus unlimited undo, PDF export, sticky notes, and multi-board sessions. Virtual whiteboards are used by teachers for online lessons, remote teams for visual meetings, students for study notes, and designers for wireframing.

How to Export Whiteboard — Save as PDF, PNG & Offline Project File
Three formats, one click each. Choose what works best for your use case.
PNG
Save Whiteboard as PNG Image
The fastest way to share your whiteboard. A PNG file keeps drawings sharp and clear — ideal for emails, Google Classroom, and inserting into presentations.
  1. 1Open the toolbar at the top of the whiteboard and click thePNGbutton.
  2. 2The export runs instantly — no dialog box, no extra clicks.
  3. 3The PNG file downloads automatically to your device'sDownloadsfolder.
  4. 4Share via email, upload to Google Classroom, or drag into any presentation.
Best for:Sharing as an image, submitting homework, inserting into Google Slides, Word, or Canva.
PDF
Export Whiteboard as PDF Document
Save your whiteboard as a clean, printable PDF. If you have multiple boards open, each board exports as a separate page in one PDF file.
  1. 1Click thePDFbutton in the top toolbar of the whiteboard.
  2. 2Your browser's print dialog opens with a preview of the board.
  3. 3Set theDestinationto "Save as PDF" in the print dialog.
  4. 4ClickSave— the PDF downloads to your device instantly.
  5. 5Open with any PDF viewer, print it, or attach it to assignments and reports.
Best for:Printing lesson notes, formal submissions, meeting summaries, and multi-page project documents.
Project File
Save Whiteboard Offline as JSON File
Want to close the browser and continue later? Save your full whiteboard project as a JSON file. Everything is preserved — drawings, shapes, sticky notes, text, colors, and layout.
  1. 1Click the⋯menu in the top toolbar, then selectSave Project.
  2. 2The whiteboard converts to a structured JSON project file automatically.
  3. 3The file downloads to your device. Save it to Desktop, Documents, or a school folder.
  4. 4To resume: open OnlineWhiteboard.org, click⋯ → Open Project, and select your saved file.
  5. 5Your entire board reloads exactly as you left it — fully editable.
Best for:Saving work in progress, creating reusable lesson templates, and keeping offline backups of important boards.Full export guide →

The terms are used interchangeably. An online whiteboard and a virtual whiteboard both refer to a digital drawing canvas that runs in your browser. OnlineWhiteboard.org is both — a free online whiteboard and a free virtual whiteboard, with no login required.
