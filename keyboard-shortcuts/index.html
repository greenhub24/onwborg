Pro Drawing Modifiers

Hold These Keys While Drawing:
  • Shift = Perfect straight lines (0°, 45°, 90° angles)
  • Shift + Shapes = Perfect circles/squares
  • Space = Temporary pan tool (hold + drag)
  • G = Grid snapping enabled
Power User Tip: P→H→E→Space→G sequence lets you draw, highlight, erase, pan, and align without touching your mouse.

Mobile & Touch Devices

  • Two-finger drag = Pan canvas
  • Pinch in/out = Zoom
  • Tap toolbar icons (same single-letter shortcuts work)
  • Double-tap canvas = Reset zoom
  • Swipe from edge = Show shortcuts (? equivalent)

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I see shortcuts while drawing?

Press ? anytime. Toggles shortcuts panel on/off instantly. Works during drawing, zooming, anything.

What does Spacebar do?

Hold Space + drag = instant Pan/Grab tool. Navigate large canvases without clicking toolbar.

How do I draw perfect straight lines?

P (Pen) + hold Shift while dragging. Creates 0°, 45°, 90° angles automatically.

Do shortcuts work on mobile/iPad?

Yes! External keyboards work perfectly. Touch gestures: two-finger pan, pinch zoom, toolbar taps.

Can I save with keyboard shortcut?

Ctrl+S / ⌘S = Export PNG instantly. Use PDF guide for PDF export.
