Why Parents and Teachers Choose This Whiteboard for Kids

Safety, simplicity, and genuine usefulness — all three at once.

🔒

Completely Safe — No Account

No login means no personal data from children is ever collected. No email, no name, no profile. The whiteboard exists only in the browser session — close the tab and nothing is stored anywhere.

👆

Touch-Friendly Controls

Large toolbar buttons that children can tap accurately on a tablet or touch screen. Finger drawing works smoothly — no stylus required, though styluses work too for older children who want more precision.

🌈

Bright Colours and Easy Tools

A full colour picker lets children choose any colour for drawing. The pen, highlighter, shapes, and sticky note tools are immediately engaging without being overwhelming — simple enough for an 8-year-old to explore independently.

🖨️

Print or Save Their Artwork

Export a child's drawing as a PNG image or PDF. Print it to hang on the wall, save it as a digital keepsake, or use it in a school portfolio. No account needed to export.

Activities Kids Love on the Whiteboard

Creative and educational activities that work well for ages 5–12.

Free drawing: Open the whiteboard and let children draw freely — animals, maps, imaginative scenes. The large canvas and colour variety makes it more engaging than paper. Export finished drawings as PNG to print or share.
Sticky note storytelling: Use colourful sticky notes to build a story — each note is a scene or character. Arrange them on the canvas in sequence and add simple drawings to illustrate. Export as PDF for a visual storybook.
Spelling practice: A parent or teacher writes spelling words in large text on the canvas. The child uses the highlighter to mark syllables, or traces over the word with the pen as a handwriting activity on screen.
Maths on the board: Write sums and equations on the canvas and have children solve them by writing their answers. Much more engaging than a worksheet — children can use shapes to draw number lines, counters, and visual representations.
Family drawing sessions: Two people at the same screen, taking turns — one draws a shape, the other adds to it. The whiteboard makes for a fun collaborative drawing game that's screen time with actual creative value.

Best Devices for Kids

The whiteboard works on every device — here's what works best by age.

📟

iPad (Recommended)

The best experience for children — large touchscreen, finger drawing, and the option of an Apple Pencil for older kids. Open in Safari, no app download needed.

💻

School Chromebook

Works in Chrome directly. Many school Chromebooks have touchscreens — children can draw with their finger on the screen. No Play Store needed.

🖥️

Family Desktop or Laptop

With a mouse or trackpad — slightly less natural for young children but perfectly usable. A drawing tablet connected to a computer gives the most pen-like experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this safe for children — does it collect any data?

Yes, it's safe. No account means no personal information is ever collected from children. All drawing data stays in the browser session and is never uploaded to any server. GDPR-friendly and safe for schools.

What age range is this appropriate for?

The simple interface works from around age 5 upward. Younger children (3–5) benefit from adult guidance with tool selection. Children 8+ can typically explore and use it fully independently.

Can I print my child's drawing?

Yes — click PNG to download the artwork as an image file, then print it from your device. Or use your browser's print function directly on the whiteboard page to print what's visible on screen.

Is there a time limit on the free use?

No time limit of any kind. Draw for 5 minutes or 5 hours — the whiteboard is available whenever you open it. No trial countdown, no feature lock after a period of use.
