Why Chromebook Users Need a Browser-Based Whiteboard

Chromebooks are built for the browser — so is this whiteboard.

Chromebooks run ChromeOS and are most powerful with browser-based tools. Many whiteboard apps require Android or desktop installation which adds friction, crashes on older Chromebook models, or isn't available through school-managed device policies.

OnlineWhiteboard.org is built for the browser. It runs perfectly in Chrome on every Chromebook — from budget school Chromebooks to the latest Chromebook Plus models. No Play Store needed, no permissions to configure, no admin approval required. Open the URL and draw.

For schools: Add onlinewhiteboard.org to your Chrome managed bookmarks and every student has instant whiteboard access on their Chromebook.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the online whiteboard work on Chromebook?

Yes — it works in Chrome browser on all Chromebook models. No Android app or Play Store installation required.

Can students use it on school-managed Chromebooks?

Yes. Since it's a browser-based tool on a standard domain, it works on managed Chromebooks without any special permissions or admin configuration.

Does it support stylus pens on Chromebook?

Yes. USI stylus and other Chromebook-compatible pens work on touchscreen Chromebooks with the online whiteboard canvas.

Can I use this instead of Google Jamboard on Chromebook?

Yes — Google Jamboard was shut down in December 2024. OnlineWhiteboard.org is a free, actively maintained Jamboard replacement that works in Chrome. See the Jamboard alternative guide →

Does it work offline on Chromebook?

The whiteboard requires an initial internet connection to load. Once loaded, basic drawing works if the connection drops, but for full reliability keep the connection active.
