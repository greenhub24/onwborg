How Teachers Use This Whiteboard in the Classroom

1

Open on Your Computer or Smartboard

Visit onlinewhiteboard.org/whiteboard/ — no login, no setup. Bookmark it for one-click access every lesson.

2

Share Your Screen

During video calls (Zoom, Google Meet, Teams), share your browser tab. Students see your whiteboard in real-time as you draw and explain.

3

Draw, Annotate, and Teach

Write equations, draw diagrams, upload images and annotate them, add sticky notes for activities, create flowcharts and mind maps.

At the end of the lesson, export the board as PDF. Upload to Google Classroom, your LMS, or email directly to students as lesson notes.

Teacher FAQ – Online Whiteboard

What is the best free online whiteboard for teachers?

OnlineWhiteboard.org is a top choice — completely free, no login or install needed, works on all browsers and devices including Chromebook and iPad. It includes drawing tools, sticky notes, image annotation, and PDF export.

Can students use it without a Google account or email?

Yes. Students need no account, no email, and no login. Just a browser. Ideal for younger students and BYOD classrooms.

How do I share my whiteboard with students?

Share your screen during a video call, export the board as a PDF and post to Google Classroom, or export as PNG and send directly. Multiple sharing methods built in.

Is this a good replacement for Jamboard?

Yes. Google shut down Jamboard in December 2024. OnlineWhiteboard.org replaces it with the same sticky notes, drawing, and image upload features — plus extras like flowchart tools, grid, and zoom. See our full Jamboard comparison →

Does it work on a smartboard or projector?

Yes. Open it in fullscreen mode on your smartboard or projector-connected computer. The canvas scales to fill the screen perfectly.

Is student privacy protected?

All data stays in the browser — nothing is collected or uploaded to servers. No student names, emails, or work are stored online. There are no accounts to compromise.
