Frequently Asked Questions

Yes — no account, no school email, no parent consent needed for sign-up. Open the URL in any browser and the whiteboard is immediately ready.

Yes — it's a standard web tool on a public domain. It works in Chrome on managed Chromebooks without any admin permissions or whitelist configuration.

Yes — click the PDF button and download. Submit the PDF via Google Classroom, email, or your school's submission portal like any other document.
