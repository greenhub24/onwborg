How Digital Sticky Notes Work

As intuitive as real sticky notes — but on an infinite digital canvas.

1

Select the Sticky Note Tool

Click the sticky note icon in the whiteboard toolbar. Your cursor changes to note-placement mode.

2

Click to Place on Canvas

Click anywhere on the canvas to place a sticky note. A note appears with a text cursor — type your idea immediately.

3

Choose a Color

Pick from multiple sticky note colors — yellow, blue, green, pink, orange. Use colors to categorize ideas, assign to teams, or indicate priority.

4

Drag to Organize

Click and drag any sticky note to reposition it. Cluster related notes together, create affinity groups, or arrange them into categories and columns.

💡 Power Tip: Combine sticky notes with the drawing tool — draw lines or circles grouping related notes together. This makes affinity mapping and idea clustering much clearer visually.

Sticky Note Activities for Teaching & Teams

Classic sticky note exercises — now digital, free, and shareable.

💡

Brainstorming Sessions

Generate ideas rapidly — one idea per note. Spread them across the canvas, then group and cluster related concepts for structured ideation.

📚

KWL Charts

Know / Want to Know / Learned. Teachers create three columns, students add sticky notes to each. A classic classroom activity made digital.

🚪

Exit Tickets

Students (or the teacher noting responses) add end-of-lesson sticky notes: "One thing I learned today" or "One question I still have."

🔄

Sprint Retrospectives

Remote agile retros: What went well / What to improve / Action items. Each category gets its own colored notes. Export as PDF for records.

🗂️

Affinity Mapping

Collect feedback or research observations on individual notes, then group them into themes by dragging and clustering. Find patterns in qualitative data.

📋

Task & Kanban Boards

Create a simple To Do / Doing / Done board with sticky notes as tasks. Move notes between columns as work progresses. Low-tech project tracking.

🎓

Vocabulary Activities

Teachers put terms on one color, definitions on another. Students match and pair them by dragging notes together. Engaging and visual vocabulary review.

🔢

Prioritization

Write options or ideas on notes, then arrange them in order of priority — top to bottom, or use a 2x2 impact/effort matrix layout.

🌡️

Mood Check-ins

Each colored note = a mood or sentiment. Quick visual check-in for team wellbeing or student engagement at the start of a session.
