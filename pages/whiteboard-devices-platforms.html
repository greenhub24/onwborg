Devices & Platforms — FAQ

Do I need to install an app?

No. OnlineWhiteboard.org runs entirely in your browser. No app store, no download, no installation on any device.

Does Apple Pencil pressure sensitivity work?

Yes. In Safari on iPad, Apple Pencil pressure sensitivity is supported for natural drawing. See the full Apple Pencil guide →

Can my students use it on their school Chromebooks?

Yes. No IT setup or admin permissions needed. Students open the URL in Chrome and use it immediately.

Does it work without internet?

The whiteboard requires an internet connection to load initially. Once loaded, drawing works even with intermittent connectivity. Saving uses your local device storage.
