The Best Browser Whiteboard for Mac

Open any browser on your Mac — your whiteboard is ready.

Mac users often default to apps like Freeform or third-party drawing tools. But for online whiteboarding — especially when sharing with others who aren't on Mac — a browser-based tool is more flexible. OnlineWhiteboard.org opens in Safari, Chrome, or Firefox with no installation and works the same on any platform.

On Mac, you get precise mouse and trackpad drawing with full multi-touch trackpad gesture support for zooming and panning the canvas. If you have a Wacom tablet or other drawing tablet connected, it works with the canvas for natural pen input.

Trackpad tip: Use two-finger scroll to pan the canvas and pinch to zoom on Mac trackpad — the same gestures as in other Mac apps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does OnlineWhiteboard.org work on Mac?

Yes — it works in Safari, Chrome, and Firefox on all Mac models running macOS 10.13 or later.

Does Mac Freeform do the same thing?

Apple Freeform is built for Apple devices. OnlineWhiteboard.org works on any device and any browser — useful when collaborating with Windows or Android users.

Can I drag images from Finder onto the whiteboard?

Yes. Drag an image file from Finder directly into the browser window and it uploads to the canvas automatically.

Does it work with a drawing tablet on Mac?

Yes. Wacom tablets and other drawing tablets work with the canvas via the browser pointer input API.

Can I use it in Zoom or Google Meet on Mac?

Yes — open onlinewhiteboard.org in a browser and share your screen in Zoom or Meet. See the Zoom guide →
