How-To FAQ

How do I save my whiteboard without an account?

Click the export button and select PNG or PDF. Your whiteboard downloads immediately — no account, no login, no watermark. Full save guide →

How do I share the whiteboard in real time?

Open the whiteboard in your browser, then share that browser tab or your screen over Zoom, Google Meet, or any video call platform. Your audience sees everything you draw live.

Can I add text to my whiteboard drawing?

Yes. Use the text tool to type anywhere on the canvas. You can also combine typed text with freehand handwriting on the same board. Text whiteboard guide →

How do I insert an image onto the whiteboard?

Click the image upload button in the toolbar, select a file from your device, and it appears on the canvas. Resize, move, or draw over it freely. Image insert guide →
