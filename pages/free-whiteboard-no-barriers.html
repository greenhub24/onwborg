Free Access — FAQ

Will it always be free with no login?

Yes. OnlineWhiteboard.org is built as a permanently free, no-login tool. There is no freemium model that could change, no trial countdown, and no plan to add a login requirement.

Is it safe to use without an account?

Yes. Because there's no account, there's nothing to be compromised. Your drawing data never leaves your browser — it's stored locally on your device only.

What's the catch?

There is no catch. The tool is supported by non-intrusive advertising shown around — not inside — the whiteboard canvas. All features remain free for all users, always.

Do I need an account to save my work?

No. Export as PNG or PDF anytime with one click — no account, no watermark. You can also save a project file to your device to reload later. Full save guide →
