Education Whiteboard — FAQ

Is the whiteboard free for schools and teachers?

Yes. Completely free. No per-seat licence, no school account, no contract. Teachers and students open the URL and use it — no setup required.

Is it COPPA-safe for use with children?

Yes. No accounts, no logins, no personal data collected. Nothing is stored on servers. Safe for K-12 students under COPPA and GDPR-compliant for EU schools.

Does it work on school Chromebooks?

Yes. OnlineWhiteboard.org runs fully in Chrome and works on any Chromebook without installation, admin permissions, or any configuration.

Can I replace Google Jamboard with this?

Yes. It has the same core features — sticky notes, freehand drawing, image upload — and is actively maintained. See the full Jamboard comparison →

Can students use it on their own devices?

Yes. It works on every device — Windows, Mac, iPad, Android, iPhone, Chromebook — in any modern browser. Students can open it at home, in class, or on mobile.
