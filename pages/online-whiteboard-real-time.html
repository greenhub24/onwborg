What Makes a Whiteboard Truly Real-Time

Speed and immediacy — no barriers between intent and drawing.

"Real-time" means two things in digital whiteboards. First, it means the tool responds instantly to your input — your pen stroke appears the moment you draw, with no rendering lag or input delay. Second, in collaborative tools, it means other participants see your drawing update live without page refreshes.

OnlineWhiteboard.org is real-time in the first sense: it is a locally-rendered canvas with zero input lag. Your pen strokes render instantly in the browser with no round-trip to a server — because all drawing is processed locally on your device. This makes it faster and smoother than cloud-synced tools where every stroke must travel to a server and back.

For live sharing — where multiple people draw simultaneously — share your screen during a video call. Your drawing appears on their screen in real time through the screen share feed, which updates continuously as you draw.

For live meetings: Open the whiteboard before your call starts. When you share your screen in Zoom or Meet, every stroke you make is visible to participants in real time through the screen share — zero additional setup.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this whiteboard real-time for multiple users?

The canvas is locally rendered — ultra-fast for the person drawing. For multiple users to see your canvas simultaneously, share your screen in a video call. They see your drawing update in real time through the screen share feed. Learn about collaboration →

Why is it faster than other online whiteboards?

Because all rendering is local — your strokes are processed in the browser on your device, not sent to a server and back. Cloud-synced tools have network latency on every stroke. OnlineWhiteboard.org has none.

Can I use it for real-time annotation of documents?

Yes — upload an image of any document to the canvas and annotate it in real time with the pen tool. Changes appear instantly on the canvas.

Does the real-time drawing work on iPad and mobile?

Yes — the local rendering approach works equally fast on iPad and Android browsers. Apple Pencil input renders with no perceptible lag in Safari on iPad.

Can I switch between boards instantly?

Yes — multiple boards load without delay. Switch between boards at any time without losing content on other boards.
