How to Insert an Image – Step by Step

1

Open the Whiteboard

Go to onlinewhiteboard.org/whiteboard/. No login or account needed. The canvas loads immediately.

2

Find the Image Upload Button

Look for the image icon or upload icon in the toolbar — it typically looks like a picture frame or a landscape icon. Click it to open the upload dialog.

3

Select Your Image File

Your device's file picker opens. Navigate to your image and click Open or Select. Supported formats include PNG, JPG, JPEG, WEBP, and GIF.

4

Image Appears on Canvas

Your image is placed on the whiteboard canvas. It may appear centered or at the top-left. You can now move it anywhere on the board.

5

Drag to Reposition

Click and drag the image to move it anywhere on the canvas. Position it exactly where you want it before annotating.

6

Resize Using Corner Handles

Select the image and drag the corner handles to resize. Drag a corner for proportional scaling, or drag edges for freeform resizing.

7

Annotate Over the Image

Select the pen, pencil, text, or shape tool from the toolbar. Draw directly over the image to annotate labels, arrows, highlights, and notes.

💡 Annotation Tip: After positioning your image, use a bright color like red or yellow for annotations so they stand out clearly against the image content.

Image Upload FAQ

How do I add an image to my online whiteboard?

Click the image upload icon in the toolbar, select your file (PNG, JPG, WEBP), and it appears on the canvas. Then drag it to position and use drawing tools to annotate.

What image formats are supported?

PNG, JPG/JPEG, WEBP, and GIF are supported. Screenshots, photos, diagrams, and illustrations all work.

Can I insert multiple images on one board?

Yes. Upload as many images as you need. Each one can be positioned and resized independently on the canvas.

Can I annotate on top of an image?

Yes. After placing the image, switch to any drawing tool and draw directly over the image. Labels, arrows, and highlights all work on top of uploaded images.

Does inserting an image upload it to the internet?

No. Images are loaded directly into your browser and are not uploaded to any server. Everything stays private on your device.

How do I insert an image on an iPad or tablet?

Tap the image upload button in the toolbar. Your tablet's file picker opens — navigate to your Photos or Files app and select the image. It appears on the canvas and you can drag it with your finger.
