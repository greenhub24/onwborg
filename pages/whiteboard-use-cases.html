Use Cases — FAQ

Can I make flowcharts without Lucidchart or Visio?

Yes. OnlineWhiteboard.org's flowchart canvas uses shapes, connectors, and arrows to build professional flowcharts — completely free, no account needed.

Is it good for UX wireframing?

Yes, for lo-fi wireframing. Use shapes, boxes, and freehand lines to sketch screen layouts quickly — no Figma or specialist tool required.

Can I annotate screenshots and images?

Yes. Upload any image and draw over it with pens, shapes, text, and arrows. Useful for design feedback, bug reports, and teaching.

Does the whiteboard work for remote team meetings?

Yes. Open the whiteboard, share your screen over Zoom or Google Meet, and your team sees everything you draw in real time. Free, no shared account needed.
