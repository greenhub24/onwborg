Why No Download Matters

Downloaded software comes with friction at every step — find the installer, check it's for your OS version, get administrator permission, wait for it to install, agree to terms, update it later. On a school Chromebook or a locked work computer, you often can't install anything at all.

A browser-based whiteboard eliminates all of that. OnlineWhiteboard.org runs entirely inside your web browser. Nothing is installed on your device. Nothing touches your file system. If your device can open a website, it can run this whiteboard — on the first visit and every visit after.

Updates happen automatically on the server side. You never run an outdated version. There's nothing to uninstall when you're done. The tool is simply there, at a URL, whenever you need it.

Works anywhere: Windows, Mac, Linux, iPad, Android, Chromebook — if it has a browser, it has your whiteboard.

No-Download Whiteboard FAQ

Does OnlineWhiteboard.org need any browser extension?

No. It runs natively in your browser with no extension, no plugin, and no add-on. All modern browsers support it out of the box.

Can I use it on a school or work computer that restricts installs?

Yes. Because nothing is installed, you need no administrator permission. As long as the computer has internet access and a browser, the whiteboard works.

Is there an app version to download?

There is no app to download — the browser version works fully on mobile and tablet. You can however add it to your home screen from Safari or Chrome for quick access, which creates a shortcut without installing anything.

Does it work on iPad without downloading the App Store version?

Yes. Open Safari on iPad, go to onlinewhiteboard.org, and the full touch-enabled whiteboard loads immediately. Apple Pencil works directly. No App Store download needed.

Is it the same tool as downloaded whiteboard apps?

It has all the same core features — drawing tools, shapes, sticky notes, image annotation, export — plus extras like multiple boards and dark mode. No download needed to access any of it.
