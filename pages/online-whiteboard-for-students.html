How Students Use the Online Whiteboard

From math homework to group projects — visual thinking made simple.

➕

Math Problem Solving

Work through equations, draw number lines, plot graphs, and solve geometry problems step-by-step. Export your working as PDF to submit.

🧠

Mind Maps & Brainstorming

Plan essays, organize ideas, and map out topics visually. Sticky notes and shapes make it easy to rearrange thoughts on the canvas.

📝

Visual Note-Taking

Take notes that mix diagrams, text, and images. Much more engaging than plain text — ideal for visual learners and complex topics.

🔬

Science Diagrams

Draw cell structures, circuit diagrams, lab setups, and chemical structures. Upload images and annotate them for homework assignments.

👥

Group Project Planning

Use the whiteboard during a video call to plan group projects together. Share your screen and draw in real-time with teammates.

📄

Submit & Share Work

Export your whiteboard as a PDF or PNG and submit directly via email, Google Classroom, or any school platform.

Student Guide: Getting Started in 3 Steps

1

Open in Your Browser

Go to onlinewhiteboard.org/whiteboard/. No login, no signup. The canvas loads immediately on any device.

2

Draw, Write, and Organize Your Ideas

Use the pen to write equations or draw diagrams. Add sticky notes for brainstorming. Upload images to annotate. Use the text tool for typed notes.

3

Save and Submit Your Work

Export as PDF to submit homework, or save the project file to continue later. Use PNG for sharing on group chats or submitting images.

💡 Study Tip: Create a separate board for each subject using the multiple boards feature. Save each one as a project file — you'll have your own organized visual study library.

Student FAQ

Is it really free? No hidden charges?

100% free. No subscription, no premium tier, no credit card. All features including export are free forever.

Do I need to make an account?

No account, no email, no signup. Just open the URL and start drawing immediately.

Can I save my work without creating an account?

Yes. Export as PNG or PDF at any time, or save your board as a project file to your device and reopen it later to continue.

Can I submit my whiteboard work for homework?

Yes. Export as a PDF or PNG and submit via email, Google Classroom, or any platform your school uses.

Is it safe to use at school?

Yes. No data is collected, no accounts are created, nothing is stored online. Everything is private in your browser.
