What "Digital Whiteboard" Means — And What This One Offers

The difference between a basic digital canvas and a full-featured whiteboard.

A digital whiteboard is a software canvas that replaces the physical classroom or office whiteboard. It runs on a screen rather than a wall. At the most basic level it means you can draw with a mouse or stylus instead of a marker. But a genuinely useful digital whiteboard goes further — it should support multiple types of drawing tools, sticky notes for organising ideas, image upload for annotating existing content, multiple boards for different topics, and the ability to save and export your work.

OnlineWhiteboard.org provides all of these features in a single browser tab — free, with no account. The canvas loads in seconds in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge on any device and operating system.

The browser advantage: Because it's browser-based, it works identically on Windows, Mac, Chromebook, iPad, and Android — the same URL, the same tools, the same experience. No version mismatches, no OS-specific bugs.

Pen, Calligraphy & Highlighter

Shapes & Lines

Rectangle, circle, triangle, diamond, polygon, and line. All support fill colour, stroke colour, and stroke width. Connect shapes with arrows to build diagrams and flowcharts.

Directional arrows with selectable arrowhead styles. Connectors that snap to shape edges and stay attached when shapes are moved — essential for flowcharts and process diagrams.

Click to place a sticky note anywhere on the canvas. Type, resize, reposition, and colour. The primary tool for brainstorming, planning, and idea organisation on a digital canvas.

Text Tool

Add typed text anywhere on the canvas. Choose font size, colour, and position independently of other elements. Use alongside shapes and sticky notes for labelled diagrams.

Frequently Asked Questions

What browsers does the digital whiteboard support?

Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and Brave on all desktop and mobile operating systems. The experience is consistent across all browsers. Chrome and Safari tend to perform best on desktop and iOS respectively.

Does it work without an internet connection?

The initial page load requires an internet connection. Once loaded, drawing and editing work offline. Saving the project file locally works offline. Export (PNG/PDF) requires the page to be fully loaded, which requires the initial connection.

Is a digital whiteboard appropriate for professional use?

Yes — the tool is used by teachers, tutors, designers, engineers, and business professionals. The drawing tools, export quality, and multiple board support are all appropriate for professional presentations and documentation.

Is my data private when using the digital whiteboard?

Yes — all whiteboard data is processed in your browser and never sent to a server. No drawing content is uploaded, no account is created, and no usage data linked to an identity is collected.
