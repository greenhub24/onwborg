Why Did Google Shut Down Jamboard?

Google officially retired Jamboard on December 31, 2024, leaving many teachers, students, and teams without their go-to digital whiteboard.

Google announced in 2023 that Jamboard would be discontinued to focus on third-party whiteboard integrations in Google Workspace. While Google Meet and Classroom continue, the beloved Jamboard web app and hardware devices were fully retired at the end of 2024. Schools and educators who built workflows around Jamboard suddenly needed a replacement — one that was free, simple, and required no account setup.

OnlineWhiteboard.org was built to fill exactly that gap. It works in any browser, requires zero sign-up, and includes all the features that made Jamboard popular for teaching and collaboration.
