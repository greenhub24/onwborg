How to Use It in Your Meeting

1

Open Before the Meeting

Go to onlinewhiteboard.org/whiteboard/. No login, loads in seconds. Prepare the canvas — add agenda items, templates, or blank boards for each topic.

2

Share Your Screen

In Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams — share your browser tab showing the whiteboard. All participants see your canvas in real-time.

3

Draw, Diagram, and Capture Ideas

Write on the board, add sticky notes for each participant's input, draw diagrams, map processes, and annotate uploaded images.

4

Export & Share After the Call

Download the board as PDF — your complete visual meeting notes. Email to all attendees or post to your project management tool.

💡 Meeting Tip: Create a separate board for each agenda item using the multiple boards feature. Switch between boards during the meeting to keep topics organized.
