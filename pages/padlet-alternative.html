Padlet vs a Drawing-First Whiteboard

Padlet is useful, but it's designed around a different kind of board.

Padlet is popular in education for its visual board format. Its plans, limits, and features can vary over time, so check Padlet's website for current details. It's built mainly around posts on a board rather than a freehand drawing canvas.

OnlineWhiteboard.org offers a different but complementary experience — a proper drawing canvas with sticky notes, freehand pen, shapes, image upload, and clean PDF export. No account required, no board limits, and no branding added to anything you create or export.

Great for: Lessons where you need freehand drawing alongside sticky notes on one canvas.
