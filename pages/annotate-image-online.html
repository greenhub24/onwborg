How to Annotate an Image Online – 4 Steps

No account, no install — annotate any image directly in your browser.

1

Open the Free Annotation Tool

Visit onlinewhiteboard.org/whiteboard/. No login, no signup, no install. The canvas opens immediately in any browser.

2

Upload Your Image

Click the image upload icon in the toolbar. Select your PNG, JPG, WEBP, or screenshot. The image appears on the canvas — drag it to position, resize with corner handles.

3

Draw Your Annotations

Choose your annotation tool: Pen for freehand marks, Arrow/Line to point at specific areas, Text for labels and captions, Shapes to highlight or circle areas, Highlighter for emphasis.

4

Export Your Annotated Image

Click Export in the toolbar. Choose PNG to save as an image or PDF for a document. Downloads to your device instantly — ready to share or submit.

💡 Annotation Tip: Use a bright contrasting color (red or yellow) for your annotations so they stand out clearly against any image background. Use the text tool to add numbered labels for complex diagrams.

Annotate Image Online – FAQ

How do I annotate an image online for free?

Go to onlinewhiteboard.org/whiteboard/, click the image upload button, select your image, then use pen, text, arrow, or shape tools to annotate. Export as PNG or PDF for free. No login needed.

Can I annotate a screenshot online?

Yes. Take a screenshot on your device (Print Screen, Cmd+Shift+4 on Mac, or Windows+Shift+S), save it as PNG, then upload and annotate it in the whiteboard. Works perfectly for annotating web pages, documents, and software UI.

Is my image private? Does it get uploaded to the internet?

Your image is never sent to any server. Everything is processed locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your image stays completely private on your device — only you have access to it.

Can I annotate images on iPad or phone?

Yes. OnlineWhiteboard.org is touch-enabled. On iPad, you can use your finger or Apple Pencil/stylus to draw annotations. Works on Android tablets and phones too.

What's the best way to annotate a diagram for school?

Upload the diagram image, use the text tool to add numbered labels, then draw arrows from the numbers to the corresponding parts. Use a consistent color (like red) for all labels. Export as PNG to submit via Google Classroom or email.

Can I annotate multiple images on the same board?

Yes. Upload as many images as you need. Each one can be independently positioned and resized on the canvas. You can annotate across multiple images in the same session and export everything together.
