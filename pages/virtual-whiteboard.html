What Is a Virtual Whiteboard?

A virtual whiteboard is a digital canvas that replicates a physical whiteboard — but it lives entirely in your browser. Instead of standing at a physical board with markers, you open a URL and draw with your mouse, touchpad, or touchscreen. Everything you can do on a real whiteboard — draw, write, erase, add images — you can do on a virtual whiteboard, plus much more: export your work, save it, zoom in, add multiple boards, and use it from anywhere in the world.

Virtual whiteboards are used by teachers for online lessons, remote teams for visual meetings, students for study notes, designers for wireframing, and individuals for creative thinking. OnlineWhiteboard.org is one of the simplest and most capable free virtual whiteboards available — with no installation, login, or cost.

Virtual Whiteboard FAQ

What is a virtual whiteboard?

A virtual whiteboard is a digital drawing canvas you use in a browser. It works like a physical whiteboard — draw, write, erase — but with extra features like undo, image upload, sticky notes, and PDF export. No physical board or markers needed.

What is the best free virtual whiteboard with no login?

OnlineWhiteboard.org — completely free, no login, no install. Open in any browser and start drawing immediately. Full feature set including drawing tools, sticky notes, image upload, multiple boards, and PDF export.

Does a virtual whiteboard need to be downloaded?

No. OnlineWhiteboard.org runs entirely in your browser. Nothing to download, install, or update — ever.

Can I use a virtual whiteboard offline?

You need an internet connection to load the page initially. Once loaded, drawing works without interruption. Your work is saved locally in your browser.
