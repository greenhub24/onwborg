Privacy & Safety for Schools

OnlineWhiteboard.org was built with privacy as the foundation, not an afterthought. When students and teachers use the whiteboard, absolutely no personal data is collected — no names, no emails, no school information, no IP logs tied to user identity.

All drawing and board data is processed entirely in the browser using JavaScript. Nothing is transmitted to external servers. When a student closes their browser, their whiteboard session is gone — nothing persists on our servers. Schools do not need to sign a data processing agreement because there is no data to process.

This makes OnlineWhiteboard.org exceptionally safe for K-12 environments where student privacy laws (like COPPA, FERPA, GDPR) apply. No accounts means no privacy exposure. No data collection means no breach risk.

For IT Administrators: OnlineWhiteboard.org requires only that JavaScript is enabled and the domain is not blocked. No ports, no installs, no data agreements. Safe-list onlinewhiteboard.org in your web filter and it's ready to use school-wide.
