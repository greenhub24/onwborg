Tips for Drawing Great Diagrams

Enable Grid View: Turn on the grid before you start. It makes aligning shapes and keeping consistent spacing much easier — especially for flowcharts and org charts.
Use Color Coding: Assign colors to shape types (e.g., blue for start/end, yellow for processes, red for decisions in flowcharts). Consistent color coding makes diagrams instantly readable.
Label Everything: Use the text tool to label all shapes and connectors. Diagrams with labeled arrows are much clearer than those relying on position alone.
Save Frequently: Export a PNG or save your project file regularly as you build complex diagrams. This protects your work from accidental browser closes.

Diagram Whiteboard FAQ

Yes. OnlineWhiteboard.org includes all the tools for diagrams — shapes, lines, arrows, text labels, and grid support. Create flowcharts, mind maps, org charts, and more for free with no login.

Is this a good free alternative to Lucidchart or draw.io?

For quick, freehand-style diagrams and sketches — yes. OnlineWhiteboard.org is ideal for fast diagramming in meetings, teaching, and brainstorming. Yes. Export any diagram as PNG or PDF in one click. No watermarks, no account, no cost. PDF exports are great for presentations and formal documents.

Can I draw flowcharts on mobile?

Yes. OnlineWhiteboard.org works on phones and tablets. Touch drawing is supported. For complex flowcharts, a larger screen or tablet with stylus gives the best experience.
