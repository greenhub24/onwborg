When You Might Want a Different Whiteboard

Excalidraw focuses on simple, hand-drawn style diagrams. Different tools suit different needs.

Excalidraw is an excellent open-source whiteboard for hand-drawn style diagrams. It keeps its interface deliberately simple. If you want a tool with a broader set of built-in whiteboard features, such as sticky notes or multiple boards, an alternative may suit you better. Features change over time, so check Excalidraw's website for current details.

OnlineWhiteboard.org is a fuller-featured alternative. You get a frictionless no-login approach along with sticky notes, image upload, PDF and PNG export without a watermark, multiple boards, dark mode, laser pointer, and more — all without paying or creating an account.

Best use: Use OnlineWhiteboard.org when you need more than a simple sketching surface — for teaching, client presentations, brainstorming sessions, and annotating images.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a better alternative to Excalidraw?

It depends on your needs. OnlineWhiteboard.org includes sticky notes, image annotation, PDF export, and multiple boards alongside free drawing. If you mainly want quick, minimal sketches, Excalidraw may be all you need.

Does OnlineWhiteboard.org have the hand-drawn style like Excalidraw?

OnlineWhiteboard.org uses natural pen and calligraphy brushes. The style is slightly different — smoother — while still being freehand.

Can I export without creating an account?

Yes. PNG and PDF exports are completely free with no account required and no watermark on the download.

Is Excalidraw free?

Excalidraw is open source and offers a free version; plans and options can change, so check their website for current details. OnlineWhiteboard.org is free to use with no account.

Can I use it for diagramming like Excalidraw?

Yes. Shapes, arrows, and connectors are included for creating flowcharts and diagrams. See the diagrams guide →
