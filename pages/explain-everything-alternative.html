Why Teachers Look for an Explain Everything Alternative

App installation and subscriptions are common reasons teachers look elsewhere.

Explain Everything is a highly capable interactive whiteboard and screencasting tool popular in education. It is typically used through an app, with plans and licensing that vary for individuals and schools, so installation and cost depend on your setup.

OnlineWhiteboard.org is a free in-browser alternative. Teachers can open it in any browser on any school device — including shared Chromebooks and iPads — with no app installation, no licence fee, and no account. Draw, annotate images, use the laser pointer, and export to PDF — all for free.

Great for: Schools with shared devices, substitute teachers, quick lesson prep, and any teaching scenario where installing an app isn't practical.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is OnlineWhiteboard.org free for schools?

Yes — completely free, no licence fee, no per-user cost, no account required. Any teacher or student can open it in a browser.

Does it work on school iPads without installing an app?

Yes. The whiteboard runs fully in the browser on iPad including Safari. Apple Pencil works natively for inking. See the iPad guide →

Can I record my lesson like in Explain Everything?

Screen recording is not built into OnlineWhiteboard.org — use your device's screen recorder alongside it. Explain Everything specialises in video recording which this tool does not replicate.

Can I annotate PDF worksheets?

You can upload image versions of worksheets and annotate them on the canvas. See the image annotation guide →

Is it suitable for maths teaching?

Yes — the drawing tools, shapes, and clean canvas work well for maths diagrams and worked examples. See the maths whiteboard guide →
