A Whiteboard App Without the App

Browser-based means no Google Play, no updates, no storage used.

Android whiteboard apps often require Play Store installation, permissions, and take up storage on your device. OnlineWhiteboard.org runs entirely in Chrome or Firefox on Android — no install needed, no permissions to grant, no storage consumed beyond your browser cache.

The canvas is touch-optimised for Android — pinch-to-zoom, two-finger panning, and tap interactions all work naturally. Android styluses including Samsung S Pen work for precise drawing and annotation.

Add to Home Screen: In Chrome, tap the menu and select "Add to Home Screen" — onlinewhiteboard.org appears as an icon on your Android home screen for instant access.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the whiteboard work on Android phones and tablets?

Yes — it works on all Android devices in Chrome or Firefox. The touch interface is optimised for both phones and tablets.

Does Samsung S Pen work with this whiteboard?

Yes. Samsung S Pen and other Android styluses work directly on the canvas for precise inking.

Do I need to install an app from Google Play?

No. The whiteboard runs entirely in your browser. No installation, no permissions, no storage used.

Can I use this whiteboard on an Android tablet for teaching?

Yes — Android tablets work well for teaching with the whiteboard, especially with a stylus. See the teachers guide →

How do I add the whiteboard to my Android home screen?

In Chrome, tap the three-dot menu and select Add to Home Screen. The whiteboard appears as an icon and opens like a native app.
