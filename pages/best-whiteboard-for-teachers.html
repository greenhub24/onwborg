Teaching Features — What Makes It the Best Choice

Each feature mapped to a real classroom teaching scenario.

🔴

Laser Pointer — Direct Student Attention

The laser pointer creates a temporary glowing cursor that highlights specific parts of your diagram as you explain — the digital equivalent of pointing at the physical board. Essential for large diagrams and dense worked examples.

📋

Multiple Boards — Structured Lessons

Create one board per lesson section and switch between them in order. No scrolling, no cluttered canvas — each topic gets a fresh board. Equivalent to turning a physical whiteboard page.

🖼️

Annotate Textbook Images Live

Upload a textbook diagram, map, or worksheet image before the lesson. Annotate it in real time during teaching — circle key features, add labels, highlight specific sections.

🗒️

Sticky Notes for Student Activities

Use sticky notes for KWL charts, group brainstorming, vocabulary walls, and sorting activities. Students contribute ideas verbally; the teacher adds them to the canvas.

📄

PDF Export — Lesson Handouts in One Click

When the lesson ends, click PDF. Upload to Google Classroom. Students have a clean, watermark-free record of every diagram and worked example from the lesson.

💾

Reuse Boards Across Classes

Save your lesson board file after teaching one class. Reload it for the next class — your annotations stay, or clear them and start fresh with the same prepared structure.

📐

Subject-Specific Tools

Maths: pen for equations and grid background for graphs. Science: image upload for diagrams. English: sticky notes for essay planning. History: drawing tools for timelines.

🌙

Projector-Friendly Display

Dark mode creates a high-contrast display on projectors and smartboards — white lines on dark background, easier to read at distance than standard light mode.

📱

Works on Any School Device

Chromebook (Chrome), school iPad (Safari), Windows laptop (Edge or Chrome), teacher's Mac (Safari) — the same tool works identically on every device, in any browser.
